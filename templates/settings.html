YouTube Cookies

Cookies let you download age-restricted content and YouTube Music Premium tracks. Export them from your browser first.

Checking...

How to export cookies:
Install cookies.txt extension in Firefox, log into YouTube, click the extension icon, and export. Or use yt-dlp: yt-dlp --cookies-from-browser firefox --cookies cookies.txt
